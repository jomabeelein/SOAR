🦅 What is S.O.A.R.?

At East Central Middle School, Cardinals S.O.A.R. by demonstrating these four core values everywhere on campus:

S
Show Respect Treat others, materials, and settings with care and appropriate voice levels.
O
Own Your Learning & Behavior Take responsibility, give personal best, and come prepared.
A
Act with Integrity Do the right thing even when no one is watching; help others and be honest.
R
Rise Above Conflict Make peaceful choices, walk away from drama, and accept consequences politely.

⚖️ Administrative Equity & Inclusion Controls

Monitor equity gaps, prevent bias/favoritism, and ensure every Cardinal student is celebrated.

Active Monitoring

1. Anti-Favoritism Cap Alert

Equity Shield

Flags when a single student receives 4+ nominations in a single month to prevent recognition monopolization and encourage staff to spread praise to other students.

2. "Overlooked Students" Radar

Inclusion Guard

Identifies quiet or under-recognized students who have 0 nominations this quarter so Deans can issue positive "Staff Spotlight Quests" to teachers.

3. Bias & Deficit Language Filter

Text Audit

Flags backhanded compliments or deficit phrasing (e.g., "he was quiet for once", "didn't cause trouble today") before stories go onto public TV slides or parent SMS messages.

4. Bilingual Parent Outreach Equity

Language Access

Tracks that Spanish-speaking families receive 100% translated SMS/Letters so non-English speaking guardians are equally connected to school celebrations.
